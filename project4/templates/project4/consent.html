What this is

A study about how people tell a recommender what films they like. You will be asked about films twice, in two different ways, and then shown what the system worked out about your taste.

What you will do

Two blocks. In one, you choose which of two films you would rather watch, {% if is_demo %}{{ demo.pairwise_tasks }}{% else %}{{ full.pairwise_tasks }}{% endif %} times. In the other, you drag ten films into your order of preference, {% if is_demo %}{{ demo.ranking_tasks }} time{% else %}{{ full.ranking_tasks }} times{% endif %}. After each block there are {% if is_demo %}{{ demo.holdout_pairs }}{% else %}{{ full.holdout_pairs }}{% endif %} further comparisons and four short questions. About {% if is_demo %}three{% else %}fifteen{% endif %} minutes in total.

There are no right answers

Nothing here is a test. If you have not seen a film, go by what you would rather watch. If two feel equally appealing, pick either.

What is recorded

Which films you were shown, the order you put them in, how long each screen took, and your answers to the questions. Nothing that identifies you: no name, no email address, no IP address, and the comment box records only whether you wrote something, not what.

Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You can close the tab at any point and your partial answers will not be analysed. Because the data cannot be linked back to you, it cannot be withdrawn after you finish.

Use of the data

Anonymous responses would be used for research on preference elicitation and could be published in aggregate. In a real deployment this section would name the responsible researcher, the institution, the ethics approval number and a contact address for questions or complaints.
